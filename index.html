ABOUT ME

I'm Shaik Abdul Bakhtiar — an Automation Engineer (SME) at OSI, working on the Enfin platform, with 3+ years building automation for finance and SaaS platforms: UI and API frameworks in Playwright, Selenium and RestAssured, plus mentoring the team as its subject-matter expert.

I've been building a security skillset the same way I build frameworks: hands-on and repeatable, anchored by the CEH v13 (Certified Ethical Hacker) certification from EC-Council. That means VAPT labs with Nmap, Nessus and Metasploit, OSINT with Subfinder, Sherlock and httpx, and foundations in cryptography, Windows security, cloud security and DevSecOps. It came together in SEC-FRAME — a security-automation framework I wrote that runs security checks as BDD scenarios and wires DAST scanning through OWASP ZAP. v0.2 is in active development.

I hold a B.Tech in IT and an MBA, which mostly means I can translate technical risk into business terms — what a vulnerability costs, and what fixing it saves.
